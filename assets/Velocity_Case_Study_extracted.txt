  0 1                             PROBLEM & OPPORTUNITY

01 · PROBLEM & OPPORTUNITY

REWARDS CUSTOMERS CAN’T SEE GET
ABANDONED.
               P R O B L E M S TAT E M E N T


            Motorists who refuel at Shell in Johannesburg earn points on a card
            they rarely carry and a balance they never see. Value is hard to use, tied
            to one card or partner, and there is no digital way to tip the attendant.
            The programme loses the repeat visits it exists to earn.

      How might we make rewards visible and usable without slowing the fuel stop?

  0 7 DESIGN DECISIONS
07 · DESIGN DECISIONS

KEY DECISIONS, WITH THE SCREENS THAT
PROVE THEM.



       01 SEE IT
           DECISION          Fuel and in-store purchases feed one visible points balance.
           REJECTED          Separate fuel and shop wallets mirroring Shell’s structure.
           EFFECT         One number reinforces a “one programme” model.




                                                            02 USE IT NOW
                                                                DECISION        Redeem or withdraw close to the moment it’s earned.
                                                                REJECTED         High thresholds, delayed rewards, restricted catalogues.
                                                                EFFECT         Shortens the distance between behaviour and benefit.




       03 PAY MY WAY
           DECISION          Earning stays payment-agnostic.
           REJECTED          A bank-linked or single-card structure.
           EFFECT         Broadens participation; Shell, not a bank, stays central.




                                                            04 THANK CLEARLY
                                                                DECISION        Tipping is separate from fuel, with recipient and amount explicit.
                                                                REJECTED         A purely transactional journey; gratuity bundled invisibly.
                                                                EFFECT         Keeps the human layer while supporting a cashless future.
