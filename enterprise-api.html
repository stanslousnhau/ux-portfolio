Enterprise API Community of Practice

A discovery engagement into the human layer of an enterprise API ecosystem: why teams rebuild, what makes reuse difficult, and how a connected community could help.

RESEARCH DESIGN

Research design tree

Four investigation territories shaped the research.

THE CENTRAL RESEARCH QUESTION

What makes teams build again instead of reuse?

Understanding the organisational conditions behind API ownership, collaboration, discovery and reuse.

01
TERRITORY ONE

Ownership

Who takes responsibility for an API across its lifecycle?

  • Roles and accountability
  • Ownership handovers
  • Support and maintenance
02
TERRITORY TWO

Incentives and culture

What encourages teams to share, contribute or reuse?

  • Team priorities
  • Recognition and incentives
  • Attitudes to shared work
03
TERRITORY THREE

Collaboration

How do teams coordinate across organisational boundaries?

  • Dependencies and handoffs
  • Knowledge exchange
  • Coordination breakdowns
04
TERRITORY FOUR

Discovery and reuse

What makes an existing API easy to find and safe to adopt?

  • Search and discoverability
  • Suitability and trust
  • Reuse versus rebuilding
