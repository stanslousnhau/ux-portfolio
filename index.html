About me

I moved into UX to connect evidence with people’s everyday experiences.

I completed a BCom in Statistics at Nelson Mandela University, where I learned to examine data, recognise patterns and question assumptions. I wanted to apply that analytical foundation to problems that affect people directly, which led me to UX research and product design.

My UX training has helped me build skills in understanding user needs, planning research, synthesising findings and translating insights into product concepts and design decisions. I bring both qualitative evidence and quantitative reasoning to the work, while being clear about what the evidence can, and cannot, tell us.

I also facilitate UX research classes and research programmes, supporting learners as they practise research methods and user-centred problem-solving. Facilitation has strengthened my ability to explain complex ideas clearly, guide group discussions and help teams build shared understanding.
