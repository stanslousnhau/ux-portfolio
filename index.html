03 — About me

I moved into UX to connect _evidence_ with people’s everyday experiences. I question assumptions, spot _patterns_, and stay honest about what the evidence can, and cannot, tell us.

SNResearch. Facilitation. Strategy.

I completed a BCom in Statistics at Nelson Mandela University, where I learned to examine data, recognise patterns and question assumptions. I wanted to apply that foundation to problems that affect people directly, which led me to UX research and product design.

I also facilitate UX research classes and research programmes, supporting learners as they practise research methods and user-centred problem-solving.
